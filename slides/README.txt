Two different things live here.

1. STAGE DECKS — a PDF per stage, e.g. stage-3.pdf. Add the relative path
   to the `slides` field for that stage in media.js and a Slides link
   appears on its card. It must be a direct PDF; a Google Drive share
   link will not work.

2. REVISION-SHEET IMAGES — s-01.jpg, s-02.jpg, … shown as a strip at the
   top of the revision sheet. Add one caption per image to the RVSLIDES
   array near the top of student.js. Leave that array empty and the strip
   does not appear at all.
